About Kaylee

The neighbour who just moved in.

I'm 16, in Grade 11 at St. Mary's High School, and I just moved to McKenzie Towne. I'm looking forward to meeting my new neighbours, and their dogs, one walk at a time.

The rules are simple and I don't bend them: we meet before the first walk, I only walk in daylight, never more than three dogs at once, and you always get a photo when your dog is home.

A bit about me

Active outdoors, and genuinely an animal person.

Active outdoors, always have been

I've played volleyball since I was 14 and did ski jumping as a kid, so being active outside in any weather is second nature to me. Snow, sun, or a Calgary wind that changes its mind twice an hour, I'm out there.

I understand the bond

I had a dog of my own growing up, and I sadly lost her to cancer a few years ago. That's part of why I do this: I know exactly how much your dog means to your family, and I don't take that lightly.

Comfortable with all kinds of pets

Beyond dogs, I've spent plenty of time with bunnies, snakes, and cats too, so I'm genuinely comfortable around animals of every kind, not just the easy ones. Your dog is in trustworthy, experienced hands.

Your dog is my dog

My rule is simple: I treat every dog like my own. That means real attention on every walk, water on hot days, paw checks on icy ones, and a photo sent to you every time so you never have to wonder.

Local & accountable

I'm not an app or a franchise, I'm your neighbour. You know where I go to school, my parents know I'm doing this, and you can always call or text me directly at 587-433-2199.
