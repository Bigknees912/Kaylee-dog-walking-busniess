nice to meet you

Hi, I'm Kaylee

I'm 16, in Grade 11 at St. Mary's High School, and I've lived in McKenzie Towne pretty much my whole life. If you've walked the path around the pond, we've probably already said hi.

I grew up outdoors

Some kids grew up on screens — I grew up on the pathways. Biking, tobogganing, and endless walks around the neighbourhood. Being outside with a dog is honestly my favourite place to be, in any season Calgary throws at us.

I own a big dog myself

I have a large breed dog of my own, so I know how to handle strong pullers, big energy, and dogs that think they're lap dogs. Small dogs, giant dogs, shy dogs, goofballs — all welcome.

Your dog is my dog

My rule is simple: I treat every dog like my own. That means real attention on every walk, water on hot days, paw checks on icy ones, and a photo sent to you every time so you never have to wonder.

Local & accountable

I'm not an app or a franchise — I'm your neighbour. You know where I go to school, my parents know I'm doing this, and you can always call or text me directly at 587-433-2199.

Heads up: dog walking carries some inherent risk — leashes can slip, dogs can react to squirrels or other animals, weather happens. I take every precaution I can on every walk, but I want to be upfront about that before you book.
